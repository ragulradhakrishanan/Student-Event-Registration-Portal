About Kalloori Kalai Thiruvizha

State-level cultural arts festival of the Tamil Nadu Higher Education Department (G.O (D) No. 148) for students of 252 government colleges and technical institutions.

College level→University level→State level

Free for students. Each student can take part in up to 3 events.

  • Fine Arts: painting, drawing, lyrics writing with tunes
  • Literature: poetry, short story, essay
  • Music: solo, instrumental, group
  • Performing Arts: dance, folk arts, theatre
  • Speech & Debate: oratory, budget battle, discussion
  • Expression: stand-up comedy, monologue
  • Eco Arts: eco-themed projects and displays
  • Innovation: treasure hunt, martial arts, tech-creative showcase
